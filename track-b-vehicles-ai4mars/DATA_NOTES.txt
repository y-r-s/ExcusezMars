TRACK B  -  Vehicles & Mobility  -  AI4MARS terrain pack
====================================================

What this is for
  Terrain-aware route scoring / self-driving surface transport for a
  100-person settlement across 2 years of duty cycles (battery wear included),
  with a dust-storm season inside the window.

Source dataset
  AI4MARS (~35k rover images, ~326k terrain labels)
  NASA: https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/ai4mars-a-dataset-for-terrain-aware-autonomous-driving-on-mars
  HF mirror used for slicing: https://huggingface.co/datasets/hassanjbara/AI4MARS
  Classes: soil (0), bedrock (1), sand (2), big rock (3), unlabeled (255)

What we sliced / simulated
  SLICED: 48 real Curiosity/AI4MARS image+label pairs (resized 256x256)
  from the Hugging Face AI4MARS mirror (train split, labeled only).
  SIMULATED: 12 extra frames with simple dust occlusion for storm robustness.
  SIMULATED: logistics_duty_cycle_100p_730sol.csv
      ~4-6 trips/sol x 730 sols for 100 residents (cargo / EVA / inter-module).
      Includes terrain_risk_score, energy_kwh, payload_kg, battery_soh decay,
      and dust_storm_flag for sols 180-260.

Files
  images/          rover frames (.jpg)
  labels/          matching segmentation masks (.png)
  label_legend.json
  logistics_duty_cycle_100p_730sol.csv
  manifest.json

How to use tonight
  Segment terrain from images/labels, then score routes in the duty-cycle CSV
  (or invent a Mars train timetable) under dust_storm_flag + battery_soh.
